How was this experiment run?

No test answers enter training or parameter selection. The replay uses the first predeclared seed, rather than selecting the best-looking run.

A one-minute explanation

  1. Start: “We give the computer only two answers for each sport.”
  2. Learn: “Two readers see different words. They keep several guesses, because one guess can be wrong.”
  3. Try: “This article was kept out of training. Let's reveal whether the learned answer is right.”
  4. Compare: “These bars are our measured ranking scores. The paper's scores are shown separately.”

Keyboard: 1–4 changes chapters. Left/right changes training rounds in chapter 2. Space plays or pauses the replay.
